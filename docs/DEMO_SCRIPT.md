Use the [final preflight and exact-page checklist](FINAL_DEMO_CHECKLIST.md) before recording.

Record at https://profily-ai.vercel.app. Use only the fictional Jordan judge profile. Preflight microphone permission, audible playback and provider quota on the recording browser. Prepare tabs and a completed **actual** judge interview/report before recording; identify any cut to a previously completed session. Do not fabricate latency, transcripts, speech input, evidence or Wispr usage. Keep the final video at or below 2:00.

## Recording fallback

If microphone permission or recognition fails, show the visible error and use text. Do not label that take a successful voice demonstration; resolve browser permissions/service availability and re-record the spoken section if voice is required for your intended video. Model responses may take longer than a segment: edit honestly between actual states instead of simulating a result. Avoid exposing API keys, private candidates, unrelated tabs or transcript history. Only use a real, owner-approved Wispr usage card.
